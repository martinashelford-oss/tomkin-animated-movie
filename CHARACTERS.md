# Visual continuity bible

**Tomkin:** Compact stout ginger-and-white tabby walking upright; thick ribbed gray wool sock folded over ears like a baker's toque; lavender silk boxer-shorts cape attached with brass safety pin; bent stainless soup ladle; yellow plastic butter tub. Expressive, brave and comically self-important. No costume swaps.

**Penny:** Fourteen-year-old girl; windblown auburn curls, patterned headscarf, freckles; heavy canvas mechanic apron holding wrenches, pliers and gears; grease-stained linen rag; clever and practical. No adult-looking redesign.

**Masked hero:** Athletic acrobat with rain-soaked red-and-blue textured fabric suit, black web-pattern detailing and expressive white mechanical eye lenses; white synthetic lines from wrist mechanisms. For release, consider an original distinctive visual redesign and name. Keep mask on in combat; briefly lifted only in breakfast scene.

**Drone:** Gunmetal armored aerial sentinel, crimson optical visor, exposed sparking circuitry and steam.

**Leviathan:** Enormous barnacled mechanical crab walker with green conduits, iron claws and hydraulic pistons; the second walker is visibly larger.

**Environment:** Weathered cliffside stone lighthouse, narrow iron-and-stone catwalk, brass lantern mechanism, rocky ocean and sea spray. Storm at opening, brief sunrise, renewed peril, then clear golden ending.

**Palette:** Cold slate blue and gray for storm, crimson drone optics, emerald threat light, warm amber for rescue and final resolution.

## Production design additions (added by the production workflow)

### Original masked hero: "The Rigger" (replaces the red-and-blue web-suit concept in all generation prompts)
The concept above closely matches a protected franchise character, and `CLAUDE.md` asks for an original, distinct design. The role, story beats, acrobatics and quips are unchanged; only the look and the gadget change.

- **Suit:** weatherproof **storm-orange** suit with **charcoal quilted oilskin panels** on the shoulders, forearms and shins; a raised **sailor's-knotwork rope pattern** across the chest. No web lines, no spider motifs, no red-and-blue colour scheme.
- **Mask:** smooth **charcoal half-helmet** with two **round amber lantern lenses**. Their **iris shutters** widen and narrow to show expression (this replaces the white lenses).
- **Gadget:** **brass forearm reels** that fire **braided cream-coloured rigging lines** tipped with small folding grapple hooks. "Webs a door" becomes "lashes the door with rigging line", the "web shield" becomes a **rope-net canopy**, and the "white line" becomes a rigging line.
- **Harness and boots:** brass-buckled harness, black grip boots.
- **Mask lifted:** only in the breakfast scenes (006, 085), with the visor tipped up and the face turned away or in shadow.
- **Name in the script:** the speaker tag stays `HERO`. "The Rigger" is a working name; change it freely.

### Extra continuity (all 90 shots)
- **Scale:** Tomkin is knee-high to Penny. The Rigger is a full-height adult.
- **Lighthouse geometry:** a pale-grey granite tower with one faded rust-red band. A black iron gallery catwalk circles the glass lantern room at the top. Below that sit the keeper's room, then the spiral stair, then the generator room at the base with its iron sea door. An old iron footbridge crosses a chasm to a cliff-top generator shed. Black reef rocks lie offshore.
- **Time of day:** this replaces the "before dusk" in shot 015, which contradicts the morning sky in 072 and the breakfast in 085. The film runs from a pre-dawn storm (001–005), through a brief sunrise (006) and an emerald morning (007–010), then grey interiors (011–030) and the renewed storm and climax (031–080), to a clear golden late morning (081–090).
- **Prop states:**
  - The butter tub is knocked into the reactor in 010 and fished back up in 011; it is dented from then on.
  - Its lid has a cut notch from 047.
  - The drone core sits in a glass jar from 012.
  - Penny carries a brass storm lantern from 017.
  - Penny wears rubber insulating gloves for electrical work (062–070).
  - Tomkin wears the brass-washer medal from 084.
- **Lantern state:** working 001–012, dark 013–077 (it turns once in 048), lit 078–090.
- **Damage:** the parapet is smashed at 009. The catwalk is cracked from 073. The footbridge is gone from 067.
- **Fishing boat:** small blue hull, white wheelhouse, called the *Marigold* (the name is spoken only, never shown on screen).
