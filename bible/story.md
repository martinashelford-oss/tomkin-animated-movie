# Story canon

**Source:** "The Lighthouse at the Tippy-Toe of the World — A Most Peculiar Tale of Heroism,
Havoc & Haddock", Google Drive doc `lighthouse-tale`
(https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RiUgp_73rVzeMHlTe2_1RWLPCGzEkQAW5B1y-KsbLAw/edit).

The film's working title is **Tomkin and the Lighthouse Leviathan**. Note: the source story
contains no leviathan — the title is kept as given; no leviathan has been added to the plot.

## Adaptation notes
- Dialogue is condensed to ~2.5 words/second so each line fits its 8 s (or 7 s extension)
  segment. All catchphrases are kept: "ELF ALERT", "Marvel …" third-person narration,
  Duggie's hyphenated FAST mode and dotted SLOW mode, "Eat fish, bucket-head", "No."
- The kidnapping (told in dialogue in the source) is shown as a brief stylised flashback
  in shot 024 so the audience sees the villains early.
- Visual design details the story leaves open (Tomkin's eye colour and goggles, Holly's
  tiara, Ben/Holly at teacup height) are marked `# design choice` in `characters.yaml`.
