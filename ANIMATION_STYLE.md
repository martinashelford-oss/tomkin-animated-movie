# Animation direction — 15-minute action film

**Target:** fully animated, continuous-motion cinematic 3D CGI action-adventure (not a slideshow or static storyboard). 90 ten-second moving video shots.

## Visual language
- Stylized-realistic 3D character animation, cinematic lighting, believable fur and cloth simulation, dynamic ocean, rain, lightning, steam and sparks.
- Consistent models, facial features, costumes, props, scale, color and lighthouse layout across all shots.
- Animate acting, gestures, combat choreography, camera tracking and environmental movement in every shot.
- No freeze-frame or still-image pan substituted for generated action unless specifically approved.
- Frame target 1920×1080 at 24 fps, 16:9.

## Workflow
1. Make reference sheets for Tomkin, Penny, the masked hero, drone, leviathan and lighthouse.
2. Generate each 10-second animated video shot with a compatible video-generation service; use image-to-video with character references where supported.
3. Inspect continuity and motion; regenerate weak clips.
4. Add voice, effects and original/licensed music in an editor or pre-mix per clip.
5. Put 001.mp4 through 090.mp4 in assets/video and run the scripts.

The package does not itself generate animated footage, and video-generation access and costs are external.
